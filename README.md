## About Me

I'm **Randy Bordeaux** — **Leadership Coach for Engineers** · **Maxwell Certified Coach, Speaker & Trainer** · **Azure Cloud Architect** · **Microsoft Certified Trainer** · U.S. Navy veteran · Dallas–Fort Worth.

I build cloud platforms that hold up under pressure — and I coach technical people to lead with the same discipline. Twenty-plus years in IT, from help desk escalation and MSP team leadership to enterprise Azure architecture in regulated environments.

**Cloud & platform**
- Azure Landing Zones, Terraform-first IaC, and DevOps pipelines
- Zero Trust, governance, identity, and observability (KQL, Azure Monitor)
- Platforms that are operable and governable — not slide-deck architecture

**Leadership & people**
- Maxwell leadership principles applied to engineering teams
- Turning technical experts into influential leaders *before* the title arrives
- Mentoring from the queue to the architect chair — I've been both

> Automate what should be repeatable. Standardize what should be predictable. Build people who can run what you built.
